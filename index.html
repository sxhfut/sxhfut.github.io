MAC-Lab starts from multimedia affective computing and follows one long route through natural language processing, human-computer interaction, ubiquitous psychological computing, and embodied emotional intelligence. The lab cares about algorithms, and it also cares whether those algorithms can enter schools, healthcare, mobility, human-factors, and public-service scenarios with long-term value. MAC-Lab 从多模态情感计算出发，沿着自然语言处理、人机交互、普适心理计算和具身情感智能持续推进。我们关心算法本身，也关心算法进入学校、健康、交通、人因和公共服务场景之后，是否还能被长期使用、持续迭代并产生真实价值。

One Research Spine一条主线

From language understanding to deployable psychological intelligence从语言理解走向可部署的心理智能

MAC-Lab has followed affective computing as a long-term research route. The route began with natural language processing, grew into affective dialogue and multimodal affective computing, and now extends into ubiquitous psychological computing and embodied emotional intelligence. This continuity allows the lab's platforms, student training, and industry collaboration to accumulate on the same foundation. MAC-Lab 长期沿着情感计算这条路线往前走。团队从自然语言处理起步，进入情感对话与多模态情感计算，再延伸到普适心理计算和具身情感智能。正是这种连续性，让平台、学生培养和产学研合作能够沉淀在同一套基础之上。

01 · Ubiquitous Psychological Computing

Modeling mental states in real life, not only in questionnaires or short conversations.在真实生活中建模心理状态，而不只停留在问卷或短对话里。

Ubiquitous Psychological Computing links time, environment, personality, physiology, behavior, social relationship, and task pressure, connecting perception, profiling, assessment, early warning, and support.普适心理计算连接时间、环境、个体差异、生理行为信号、社会关系和任务压力，把感知、画像、评估、预警与支持放入同一条技术路线。

Capability to Delivery从能力到交付

Affective and human-factors problems need a complete route情感与人因问题需要完整路线

Industry partners often begin with one request: recognize emotion, measure stress, detect fatigue, assess mental state, or improve human performance. The real work is to define the scene, select reliable signals, interpret the state, design the support method, and deliver a system that can keep working after the demo. 产业伙伴常从一个具体需求开始：识别情绪、测量压力、检测疲劳、评估心理状态，或者提升身心效能。真正有用的工作还要继续向前：定义场景，选择可靠信号，解释状态含义，设计支持方法，并交付一个离开演示环境之后还能持续工作的系统。
